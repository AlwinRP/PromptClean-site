Keyboard shortcuts

Two keys. No panel needed.

Click into the chat box and press a shortcut. The text is cleaned where it is, a note says what changed, and Ctrl+Z puts it back.

Alt+Shift+X

Redact

Every name, account number, password and key in the box becomes a placeholder. Your wording stays exactly as you wrote it.

maria.gonzalez@acme.com→[EMAIL_1]

Alt+Shift+R

Redact and compress

Redacts first, then collapses repeated log lines and minifies JSON, so a long paste comes out short.

ERROR timeout × 200→1 line + a count

On a Mac, Alt is the ⌥ Option key. Change either shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Who it's for

Anyone who reads logs

Developers and business analysts make the same move when something breaks. They leak different things.

Developers

“Why is this failing?”

Pastes
Stack traces, server logs, config files
Leaks
API keys, passwords, connection strings

Business analysts and testers

“What do I write in the defect?”

Pastes
UAT errors, logs, rows copied from a spreadsheet
Leaks
Customer names, account and policy numbers, dates of birth

What it catches

The details that should stay behind

People and money

  • Customer names
  • Spreadsheet name columns
  • Emails
  • Phone numbers
  • Addresses
  • Dates of birth
  • Card numbers
  • Bank accounts
  • IBANs
  • Sort codes

Secrets

AWS, GitHub, OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe, Slack and Google keys, plus passwords, tokens and private keys.

IDs

  • Customer IDs
  • Policy numbers
  • US SSN
  • UK National Insurance
  • Aadhaar
  • PAN
  • Passport

Dashed items are suggested rather than ticked, because the AI often needs the ID to follow a failure. Detection is best-effort: a name with nothing around it, as in “Ask Priya Patel to retest”, is not caught, so read the result before you send it.
